03 · Explainable scoring

A transparent score, not a black box.

Every point can be traced to a signal, its weight, and its recency. Thresholds can be tuned by market, campaign, or product.

30% Engagement
+ 25% Relationship Strength

− Risk Penalties

Signals use a 90-day exponential time decay. Strong intent events—such as demo requests and workshop attendance—receive higher event multipliers. Scores are capped at 100 and recalculated daily.
